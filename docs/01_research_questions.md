# 01 — Research Questions

Primary question:
- Can Collaboration Causal Traces provide useful structural representation for diagnosing collaborative failures in LLM-based multi-agent systems?

Hypotheses tracked (future empirical work):
- H1: CCT improves controlled structural failure attribution.
- H2: Collaboration structure matters beyond flat logs.
- H3: Calibration is necessary and not overfit.
- H4: Post-hoc refinement adds limited or conditional value.
- H5: CCT can transfer, exploratorily, to external traces.
