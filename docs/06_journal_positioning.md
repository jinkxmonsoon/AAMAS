## Task 25 positioning update

The journal backbone is now Path B: a protocol-first benchmark and diagnostic-representation paper. The article should not be framed as a performance-superiority CCT method paper. Allowed emphasis includes full-trace representation, leakage and shortcut governance, artifact reproducibility, causal-flow representation diagnostics, and negative findings showing that naive structural scoring and simple causal-flow feature counts are insufficient. Forbidden emphasis includes baseline outperformance, robustness, calibration readiness, or validation of H1/H2.
