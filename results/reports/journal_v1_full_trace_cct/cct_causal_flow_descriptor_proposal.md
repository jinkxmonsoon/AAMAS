# Journal-v1 Full-Trace CCT Causal-Flow Descriptor Proposal

## Scope

This proposal defines prediction-view-safe descriptor candidates for a future explicitly authorized feature-revision task. No descriptor is implemented here for scoring, no weights are changed, and no proposed descriptor is compared against gold labels.

| Proposed descriptor | Definition | Required visible fields | Uses private labels? | Expected relation to CCT thesis | Leakage risk | Lexical shortcut risk | Deterministic extraction feasible? | LLM/judge required? | Future scoring status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `handoff_constraint_shift_indicator` | Flags when a downstream step restates, weakens, drops, or reverses a constraint introduced upstream. | ordered `steps`, `input_message`, `output_message`, `handoff_from`, `handoff_to`, `visible_step_notes` | no | Captures causal degradation across handoff. | Low if based only on visible text; medium if patterns encode builder templates. | Medium: constraint words may become shortcuts. | Partly feasible with deterministic lexical overlap/negation heuristics. | Prefer no; optional audit-only judge if deterministic recall is poor. | Candidate for future primary after pre-registration and leakage audit. |
| `evidence_conflict_indicator` | Flags visible disagreement between evidence items, tool output, and step output. | `evidence_items`, `evidence_used`, `tool_output`, `output_message` | no | Represents CCT evidence conflict and misalignment. | Low if no private rationale terms are used. | Medium: conflict tokens may be templated. | Feasible for explicit conflict markers; limited for implicit conflict. | Prefer no. | Candidate for future primary if deterministic. |
| `evidence_ignored_indicator` | Flags when visible evidence or tool output appears in context but is absent from the step output or downstream handoff. | `evidence_items`, `evidence_used`, `tool_output`, `output_message`, adjacent step messages | no | Represents missing evidence flow. | Low. | Medium: repeated evidence identifiers can be shortcuts. | Feasible using token/entity carryover heuristics. | No for first version. | Candidate for future primary. |
| `downstream_reference_to_prior_output` | Flags whether later steps explicitly depend on, quote, paraphrase, or operationalize prior output. | ordered `steps`, prior `output_message`, downstream `input_message`/`output_message` | no | Captures propagation of earlier content. | Low. | Medium: formulaic downstream phrasing can shortcut. | Feasible using lexical/entity overlap and temporal adjacency. | No. | Candidate for future primary after variance audit. |
| `tool_output_alignment_mismatch` | Flags mismatch between tool output and step output/action. | `tool_call`, `tool_output`, `output_message` | no | Captures tool-evidence misuse. | Low if tool outputs are visible prediction inputs. | Medium: mismatch markers may be scenario-specific. | Partly feasible with deterministic contradiction/omission patterns. | Optional diagnostic judge only if deterministic version insufficient. | Primary only if deterministic and audited; otherwise diagnostic. |
| `recovery_opportunity_visible_indicator` | Flags visible opportunity to correct or recover before terminal outcome, without using private recovery labels. | ordered `steps`, `visible_step_notes`, `output_message`, downstream messages | no | Supports recoverability analysis from visible trace structure. | Medium: must avoid private `recovery_opportunity`. | Medium/high: recovery language may be templated. | Feasible only for explicit visible correction opportunities. | Prefer no. | Diagnostic first. |
| `correction_attempt_visible_indicator` | Flags visible attempted correction, verification, or rollback in later steps. | downstream `input_message`, `output_message`, `tool_output`, `visible_step_notes` | no | Captures refinement/recovery behavior without private labels. | Low/medium depending on vocabulary. | Medium. | Feasible with rule lists for correction/verification terms. | No for first version. | Diagnostic first; possible primary after audit. |
| `unresolved_caveat_carryover` | Flags caveats that persist or propagate without resolution across steps. | `output_message`, `visible_step_notes`, downstream messages | no | Represents unresolved uncertainty propagation. | Low. | Medium: caveat words may shortcut. | Feasible with caveat-term carryover and resolution-term checks. | No. | Candidate for future primary after variance audit. |
| `semantic_alternative_collision_indicator` | Flags steps where competing interpretations, dates, sources, or constraints are conflated. | `input_message`, `output_message`, `tool_output`, evidence fields | no | Captures semantic collision failure mode. | Low/medium; must not use scenario label. | Medium/high if exact scenario vocabulary dominates. | Feasible for explicit paired alternatives; limited otherwise. | Prefer no; diagnostic judge possible. | Diagnostic first. |
| `cross_agent_dependency_indicator` | Flags when a step's output is required by a later agent or relies on a prior agent's output. | ordered `steps`, `agent_id`, handoff fields, adjacent messages | no | Represents causal dependency across agents. | Low. | Medium due to fixed agent order. | Feasible if normalized to avoid pure position. | No. | Candidate for future primary if position-normalized. |

## Descriptor-design guardrails

- Descriptors must be extracted only from sanitized prediction-view fields.
- Descriptors must not use `private_labels`, gold labels, label rationales, H6 private evidence, provenance, `scenario_group`, `perturbation_type`, `trace_id`, or `case_id` as scoring features.
- Each descriptor needs its own variance, leakage, shortcut, and reviewability audit before it can be considered for primary scoring.
- Proposed descriptors should first be generated as reviewable samples and compact reports, not immediately used for scoring.
