# CCT Representation Redesign Claim Boundary

## Allowed claims

Task 20 permits only the following claims:

- A specification exists for candidate richer non-position causal-flow edge types.
- The specification separates representation edges from descriptors/features and scoring rules.
- Anti-leakage, anti-shortcut, and future validation constraints have been documented.
- The edge specification is ready for a future sample-only prototype.
- Edges remain not ready for scoring.

## Forbidden claims

The following claims are not allowed from Task 20:

- CCT scoring improved.
- CCT outperforms non-CCT baselines.
- Edge extraction works on the full corpus.
- Edge features reduce duplicate graph signatures in observed data.
- Edges support H1, H2, or H6 empirically.
- Calibration is now justified.
- Descriptor-augmented scoring is justified.
- Any paper-ready result has been produced.

## Hypothesis boundary

No hypothesis is tested in Task 20. H1 and H2 remain unsupported under the current frozen uncalibrated scoring/features. H6 remains open but unsupported by scoring evidence. Task 20 only prepares a future representation-validity reassessment.
